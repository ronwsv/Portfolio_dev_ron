Featured project · AI authorship

Atas · From assembly audio to formal minutes

Writing up meeting minutes used to eat almost a full day of a property manager's work: listening back to an entire three-to-four-hour assembly recording, reconstructing who said what, and taking notes by hand on what mattered. I built the platform's AI layer to tackle exactly that step — today the manager starts from an already-structured draft instead of a blank page.

A two-phase async pipeline — transcription with speaker separation, then structuring into the required formal format — where the transcript stays reusable without reprocessing the audio. Plus a collaborative editor with an assistant that consults the condo's own documents: instead of stuffing context into the prompt, the model gets a search tool and decides on its own when to use it, returning the file and passage that back each answer. Each condo has its own isolated context.

Growing adoption: from zero to around ten assemblies transcribed automatically per month, over six months.

SvelteKit · Tiptap · Flask · Celery · Redis · GPT-4o · AssemblyAI · Docker
